Transfer admission requirements

Transfer applicants need a college GPA of 2.5 or higher and good standing at the most recent institution. Send official transcripts from every college attended; if you have fewer than 24 college credits, also send a high school transcript.

The transfer application fee is $65. Apply by March 1 for Fall or October 15 for Spring.

How credits transfer

Tondro accepts up to 90 transfer credits from four-year institutions and up to 64 from community colleges. Courses with a C or better in subjects we offer generally transfer; the Registrar posts a preliminary credit evaluation about two weeks after admission.

We hold articulation agreements with Harbor Falls Community College, North Shore College and Riverbend Technical College — courses on those agreements transfer automatically.

Transfer scholarships

Transfers are automatically reviewed for merit awards of $6,000 to $20,000 per year. Phi Theta Kappa members receive a $2,000 annual scholarship on top of any merit award.

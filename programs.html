Degrees and majors

Programs of study

Undergraduate programs

Tondro offers 42 undergraduate majors across four colleges. The most applied-to majors are Nursing, Business Administration, Computer Science, Biology and Psychology.

CollegeExample majors
College of Arts & SciencesBiology, Chemistry, English, History, Mathematics, Psychology
College of BusinessAccounting, Business Administration, Finance, Marketing
College of Engineering & ComputingComputer Science, Cybersecurity, Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering
College of Health SciencesNursing, Exercise Science, Public Health

Every undergraduate completes the Tondro Core — 42 credit hours of writing, quantitative reasoning, humanities, sciences and a first-year seminar. Undeclared students enter through the Explore program and must declare a major by the end of the second year.

Graduate programs

Tondro offers 18 graduate programs. Flagship programs include the MBA (36 credits, evening and online), MS in Computer Science (30 credits), MS in Nursing (39 credits), MA in Counseling (60 credits) and Master of Education (33 credits).

Graduate tuition is $1,420 per credit hour. Most master's programs admit for Fall and Spring; the MBA also admits in Summer.

Minors and combined degrees

35 minors are open to all undergraduates. Juniors with a 3.4 GPA or higher can apply to a 4+1 combined bachelor's/master's in Business, Computer Science or Public Health, finishing both degrees in five years.

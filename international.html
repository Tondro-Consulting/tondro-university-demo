International admission requirements

International applicants follow the same steps as first-year or transfer applicants, plus proof of English proficiency: TOEFL iBT 79, IELTS 6.5, or Duolingo English Test 105 minimum. The requirement is waived for students who completed three years at an English-medium school.

Transcripts in languages other than English need a certified translation; a course-by-course evaluation (WES or ECE) is recommended but not required for admission.

Costs and certification of finances

The international application fee is $85. Before the I-20 is issued, students submit a Certification of Finances showing $63,500 for the first year. International students are eligible for merit scholarships of $8,000 to $22,000 and may work on campus up to 20 hours per week.

Visas and arrival

After paying the $400 enrollment deposit, the International Student Office issues the I-20 within two weeks. F-1 students attend International Orientation, held the week before New Student Orientation each term.

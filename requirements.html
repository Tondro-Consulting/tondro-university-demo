First-year admission requirements

First-year applicants need a completed online application, an official high school transcript, and a personal essay of 400 to 650 words. One letter of recommendation from a teacher or counselor is encouraged but not required.

There is no minimum GPA, but the middle 50% of admitted first-year students hold a GPA between 3.2 and 3.8. Tondro is test-optional through Fall 2027: SAT or ACT scores are not required, and applicants who do not submit them are not disadvantaged.

Graduate admission requirements

Graduate applicants need a bachelor's degree from a regionally accredited institution, official transcripts from every college attended, two letters of recommendation, and a statement of purpose. The MBA and MS Computer Science require a minimum 3.0 undergraduate GPA; other programs review GPA in context.

GRE scores are not required for any graduate program. The MA in Counseling adds a faculty interview.

The personal essay

Write 400 to 650 words on any of these prompts: a challenge you worked through; a community you belong to; a topic you could teach for an hour; or a moment that changed how you learn. Essays are read for voice and reflection, not polish.

Interviews

Undergraduate admission interviews are optional and informational, offered on campus or by video from September through January. Graduate interviews are required only for the MA in Counseling.

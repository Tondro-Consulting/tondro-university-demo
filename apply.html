How to apply, step by step

  1. Create an account on the Tondro application portal (or use the Common Application — we accept both).
  2. Complete the application and upload your personal essay.
  3. Pay the application fee: $65 for domestic applicants, $85 for international applicants. Fee waivers are granted automatically to students with an SAT/ACT waiver or a counselor's note.
  4. Send official transcripts directly from your school to admissions@tondro.edu or by mail.
  5. Optional: send SAT/ACT scores and one recommendation letter.

Decisions arrive by email and in the portal about four weeks after a file is complete.

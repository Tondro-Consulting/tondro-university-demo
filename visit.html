Visit campus

Daily visits and tours

Campus tours run Monday to Friday at 10:00 a.m. and 2:00 p.m., and select Saturdays at 11:00 a.m. A visit includes a 30-minute admissions presentation and a 90-minute student-led tour. Register at visit.tondro.edu — walk-ins are welcome if space allows.

Preview days and admitted-student events

Fall Preview Days: October 3 and November 14, 2026. Admitted Student Days: March 20 and April 10, 2027. Each event includes class visits, lunch in the dining hall, and parent sessions.

Virtual visits

Live virtual info sessions run every Wednesday at 7:00 p.m. Central; virtual campus tours are available on demand. To arrange a counselor call in your time zone, email visits@tondro.edu.

Getting here

The Office of Admissions is in Founders Hall, 1 University Way, Harbor Falls. Free visitor parking is in Lot A behind Founders Hall. Harbor Falls Regional Airport (HRF) is 20 minutes away; a campus shuttle runs to downtown hotels.

Your next steps

After you’re admitted

Confirm your place

Pay the $400 enrollment deposit in the applicant portal by May 1 (or two weeks after your decision, if admitted later). The deposit is refundable until May 1 and counts toward tuition. Housing assignments process in deposit order.

Key dates

  • Enrollment deposit: May 1 ($400)
  • Orientation: August 18–20, 2027 (Fall) or January 8, 2027 (Spring)
  • Housing application opens March 15
  • Final transcript by July 15; immunizations by August 1

Housing & Residence Life

  • housing@tondro.edu
  • (555) 555-0191

Orientation

New Student Orientation is required for all new undergraduates: August 18–20, 2027 for Fall entry, January 8, 2027 for Spring entry. Registration opens April 1 and costs $95, billed with tuition. Families are welcome on day one.

Housing

First-year students live on campus. The housing application opens March 15 in the portal; assignments are released in early July. Standard double rooms are $12,400 per year; single rooms are $14,900 when available. Renters insurance is recommended, not required.

Housing questions: housing@tondro.edu or (555) 555-0191.

Before classes start

  1. Send your final transcript by July 15.
  2. Submit immunization records by August 1.
  3. Register for Orientation by June 15.
  4. Watch for your Tondro email account in June.

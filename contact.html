Office of Admissions, Tondro University, 1 University Way, Harbor Falls, IL 60430. Send official transcripts to admissions@tondro.edu (electronic) or the mailing address above.

How fast we reply

Email replies within two business days. Phones are staffed during office hours; after-hours calls go to voicemail returned the next business day. Admission decisions are never given by phone or chat — check your applicant portal.

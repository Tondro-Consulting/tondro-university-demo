Tondro at a glance

Students

6,800 students — 5,400 undergraduate, 1,400 graduate. Average class size 19.

Programs

42 undergraduate majors and 18 graduate programs across four colleges.

Campus

120-acre campus on the Harbor Falls riverfront, 40 minutes from downtown.

Outcomes

94% of the Class of 2025 were employed or in graduate school within six months.

How to start

  1. Explore our undergraduate and graduate programs.
  2. Check the admission requirements for your applicant type.
  3. Apply online by the deadline on the Apply & Deadlines page.
  4. File the FAFSA and CSS Profile by February 1 for priority financial aid.

Financial aid basics

File the FAFSA (school code 001842) and the CSS Profile by February 1 for priority consideration. 92% of Tondro undergraduates receive some financial aid; the average aid package is $31,200.

Tondro meets full demonstrated need for admitted first-year students who file both forms on time.

Merit scholarships

Every admitted first-year and transfer student is automatically reviewed for merit scholarships of $8,000 to $28,000 per year — no separate application. The top awards, the Harbor Falls Scholarship (full tuition) and the President's Scholarship ($28,000), require an additional essay by January 15.

Scholarships renew for four years with a 3.0 GPA and full-time enrollment.

Aid deadlines and contacts

FAFSA + CSS Profile priority deadline: February 1. Aid offers are released with admission decisions for Early Action and by mid-March for Regular Decision.

Questions: email finaid@tondro.edu or call (555) 555-0177.
